# Sign Magnitude 

---
For an n-bit number : 

| Sign (1 bit) | Magnitude (n-1 bit) |
| ------------ | ------------------- |
Sign is : 
- 0 → +ve 
- 1 → -ve 

- The Sign bit is also known as MSP (Most Significant Bit)

> [!example] 
> (+5) in binary is (`0101`)   
> (-5) in binary is (`1101`)

---
Zero can be represented in two ways : 

- +0 → `0000`
- -0 → `1000`

---
> [!note] 
> Don’t confuse [[1’s Complement]] with [[Sign Magnitude]] or [[2’s Complement]].   
> They are different ways of representation. Hence the values can be different.
