# Coaxial Cable / Coax 

- Carries signals of higher [[Transmission Media#Frequency|frequency]] ranges than those in [[Twisted-pair Cable]]
- Coax has a central core conductor of solid or stranded wire (Copper) → Enclosed in an insulating sheath → encased in an outer conductor of  metal foil, braid or a commbination of the two 
- The outer metallic wrapping serves as :
	- shield against noise 
	- second conductor which completes the circuit 
- The outer conductor is also enclosed in an insulating sheath 
- The whole cable is protected by a plastic cover

## Coaxial Cable Standards 

- Categorized by their radio government (RG) ratings
- Each RG number denotes a unique set of physical specifications 
	- the wire gauge of the inner conductor 
	- the thichness and the type of the inner insulator 
	- the construction of the shield 
	- type of the outer casting
- Each RG rated cable has a specialized function

## Connectors 

- Most common type → Bayone-Neil-Concelman (BNe)
	- BNC Connector 
		- used to connect the end of the device (such as a TV Set)
	- BNC T Connector 
		- used in Ethernet networks to branch out to a computer or other device
	- BNC Terminator 
		- used at the end of the cable to prevent the reflection of the signal

## Performance 

- The [[Transmission Media#Attenuation|attenuation]] is much higher than in [[Twisted-pair Cable]]
- The [[Transmission Media#Bandwidth|bandwidth]] is much higher, but the signal weakens rapidly and requires frequent use of repeaters

## Applications 

- Was widely used in Analog Telephone Networks 
	- Could carry 10000 voice signals 
	- Now [[Fiber Optic Cable]] are used instead 
- Was widely used in Digital Telephone Networks 
	- Could carry data up to 600 Mbps 
	- Now [[Fiber Optic Cable]] are used instead 
- previously cable TVs use coax cables 
	- RG-59 coax cables
	- Now [[Fiber Optic Cable]] are used instead 
- Hybrid networks use coax cables only at the network boundaries, near the consumer premises 
- used in early traditional Ethernet LANs 
	- high [[Transmission Media#Bandwidth|bandwidth]] consequently high [[Transmission Media#Data rate|data rate]]
	- 10Base-2 or Thin Ethernet 
		- RG-58 coax cable with BNe [[#Connectors]]
		- 10 Mbps 
		- 185 m range 
	- 10Base5 or Thich Ethernet 
		- RG-11 coax cable with specialized [[#Connectors]]
		- 10 Mbps
		- 5000 m range
