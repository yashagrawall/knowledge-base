# Digital Computers 

---
- Digital : information represented using discrete values 
- The first electronic digital computers 
	- developed in late 1940s 
	- used primarily for numerical computation : Hence the name Digital Computer
- Digital Computers use Binary Number System (0 and 1)
- A binary digit is called a bit 

> [!note] 
> The same group of bits can mean different 
> - 1001011 → 75 
> - 1001011 → K 
> 
> Same alphabets used for French and English but the words mean different
