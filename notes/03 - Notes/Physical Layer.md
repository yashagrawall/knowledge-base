# Physical Layer 

- Responsible for carrying individual bits in a frame across the link 
- Lowest Level in the [[TCP-IP Protocol]] 
- Communication between two devices at the physical layer is a [[Protocol Layering#Logical Connections|logical connection]] because there is another, hidden layer, the [[transmission media]], under the physical layer.
- Two devices are connected by a [[Transmission Media|transmission medium]] ([[Transmission Media#Types of Transmission Media|cable or air]]) 
- [[Transmission Media]] carries electrical or optical signals, not bits 
- Bits recieved in a frame from the [[Data-link Layer]] are transformed and sent through the [[Transmission Media]]
- Logical Unit between two physical layers in two devices is a bit 
- Several protocols to transform a bit → signal
