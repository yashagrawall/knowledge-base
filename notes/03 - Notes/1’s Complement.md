# 1’s Complement 

- In 1’s Complement : 
	- 1 → 0 
	- 0 → 1 

- 1’s Complement of any no. gives it -ve no. 

> [!example] 
> (+5) = (`0101`)  
> (-5) = (`1010`)

> [!note] 
> Don’t confuse [[1’s Complement]] with [[Sign Magnitude]] or [[2’s Complement]].   
> They are different ways of representation. Hence the values can be different.  

- If a no. starts with 
	- `1` → -ve no.
	- `0` → +ve no.  
- Find the 1’s Complement of a -ve no. to get the magnitude of that no. , then add the -ve sign to get the original no. 

> [!note] 
> 1’s Complement changes the sign, so in order to find the value of the original no. multiply it with -1

> [!example] 
> 
> (`11001`)     
> 1’s Complement = (`00110`) = (`6`)   
> $∴$ the original no. is `-6`

---

Zero can be represented in two ways :
- `+0` → (`0000`)
- `-0` → (`1111`)
