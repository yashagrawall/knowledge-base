# Guided Transmission Media 

- Provides a conduit from one device to another 
- Includes the Wired Transmission Media 
	- Metallic (Copper) Cables : accepts and transports signals in the form of electric current 
		- [[Twisted-pair Cable]] 
		- [[Coaxial Cable]] 
	- [[Fiber Optic Cable]] : accepts and transports signals in the form of light
