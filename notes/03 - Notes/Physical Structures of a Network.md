# Physical Structures of a Network 

---
## Type of Connection  

---
### Point-to-point 

- a dedicated link between two devices 
- can be a wire, microwave or satellite links 

![[Pasted image 20260913194401.png]]

---
### Multipoint / Multidrop 

- more than two device share a single link 
- the capacity of channel is shared, either spatially(simultaneously) or temporally(taking turns → timeshared)
