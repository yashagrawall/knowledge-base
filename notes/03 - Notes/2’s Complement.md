# 2’s Complement 

- Take the [[1’s Complement]] of the no. and add a `1` to get the 2’s Complement of that no. 

> [!note] 
> Don’t confuse [[1’s Complement]] with [[Sign Magnitude]] or [[2’s Complement]].   
> They are different ways of representation. Hence the values can be different.  

> [!example] 
> `+5` = (`0101`)   
> [[1’s Complement]] of `+5` is (`1010`) ⇒ `-5` = (`1010`)   
> 2’s Complement of `+5` is 1’s Complement of `+5` + 1   
> ⇒ `1010` + `1` = `1011`   
> ⇒ `-5` = (`1011`)

---

- To find the 2’s Complement of a -ve no. follow the same steps 

> [!note] 
> 2’s Complement changes the sign, so in order to find the value of the original no. multiply it with -1

---
Here zero is represented as : `0000`
- 2’s Complement eliminates having two representation for zero
