# Confidence

---
- Believe in yourself 
- a little bit of narcissism is good for you  
- don't overthink and compare  
	- Other are different and don't look at their age and think that they achieved so much in such a small age, its too late for me, etc  
	- they are in different position in life, instead of comparing, learn from them how they did what they did 
- Journalism / keeping track: best way to keep a track of everything is to write it down, best way to motivate yourself into achieving any goal  
	- it tell you how far have you come  
	- it also help others to learn from your path (if you plan to make it public)
- don't think less of yourself
	- if someone else can do it, then you can too 
	- if no one has ever done it before, then be the first one : 
		- reminds of a quote : 
> [!quote] 
> _Don’t Follow your Dreams_
> _Dream to be Followed_
