# Computer Networks — Regular Examination 2022–23

> **Course**: Computer Networks (PCCS4307)  
> **Programme**: B.Tech CSE — 5th Semester  
> **Time**: 03 Hours | **Maximum Marks**: 60  
> **Instructions**: Answer Question No.1 which is compulsory and any five from the rest. The figures in the right-hand margin indicate marks.

---

## [[CN Theory Syllabus]]

- **Module 1**: Network Topologies, Multiplexing (TDM), Line Coding (MLT-3).
- **Module 2**: Error Detection (CRC), Channelization (CDMA).
- **Module 3**: CIDR Addressing, Distance Vector Routing, IPv4 Datagram Header.
- **Module 4**: UDP vs TCP, TCP Congestion Control, E-Mail Architecture.
