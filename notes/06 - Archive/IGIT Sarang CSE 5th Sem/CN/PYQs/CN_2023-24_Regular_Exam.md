# Computer Networks — Regular Examination 2023–24

> **Course**: Computer Networks (PCCS4307)  
> **Programme**: B.Tech CSE — 5th Semester  
> **Time**: 03 Hours | **Maximum Marks**: 60  
> **Instructions**: Answer Question No.1 which is compulsory and any five from the rest. The figures in the right-hand margin indicate marks.

---

## [[CN Theory Syllabus]]

- **Module 1**: Layered Architecture, OSI Reference Model vs TCP/IP, Transmission Media, MLT-3, Pulse Code Modulation (PCM).
- **Module 2**: CSMA/CA, CDMA & Walsh Codes, PPP Transition Phases.
- **Module 3**: Subnetting, Routing Algorithms (Link State Routing).
- **Module 4**: Transport Protocols (UDP vs TCP), E-Mail Architecture (MIME), DNS, Packet Switching, CRC.
