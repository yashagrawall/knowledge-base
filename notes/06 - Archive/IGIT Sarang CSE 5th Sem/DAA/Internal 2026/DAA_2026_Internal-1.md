## [[DAA Theory Syllabus]]

- **Module 1**: Asymptotic analysis, Recurrence relations (Master theorem, substitution method, recursion tree).
- **Module 2**: Sorting & searching algorithms, Heapsort (except hashing).
- **Module 3**: Algorithm design techniques (mostly greedy and dynamic programming: LCS, Job sequencing with deadlines, Fractional Knapsack) and Graphs.
