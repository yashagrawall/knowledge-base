> **Programme**: B.Tech (Computer Science & Engineering)  
> **Semester**: 5th Semester Regular 2nd Terminal 2026

## [[MPMC Theory Syllabus]]

- **Module 2**: 8086 Microprocessor (BIU & EU Architecture, Minimum & Maximum Mode, Dedicated Interrupts Type 0 to 4).
- **Module 3**: Peripheral Interfacing (8255 PPI, 8251 USART, 8259 PIC, 8237 DMA Controller).
- **Module 4**: 8051 Microcontroller Architecture, Pin Configuration, and Port Structure.
