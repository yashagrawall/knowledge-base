# Microprocessor & Microcontroller — Regular Examination 2022–23

> **Course**: Microprocessor and Microcontroller (PCCS4308)  
> **Programme**: B.Tech CSE — 5th Semester  
> **Time**: 03 Hours | **Maximum Marks**: 60  
> **Instructions**: Answer Question No.1 which is compulsory and any five from the rest. The figures in the right-hand margin indicate marks.

---

## [[MPMC Theory Syllabus]]

- **Module 1**: 8085 Architecture, Machine Cycles, Opcode Fetch Timing Diagram, Interrupts.
- **Module 2**: 8086 Architecture, Segmentation, Min/Max Mode.
- **Module 3**: Peripheral Interfacing (8255 PPI, 8251 USART, 8237 DMA Controller).
- **Module 4**: 8051 Microcontroller Architecture and Port Structure.
