# Microprocessor & Microcontroller — Regular Examination 2023–24

> **Course**: Microprocessor and Microcontroller (PCCS4308)  
> **Programme**: B.Tech CSE — 5th Semester  
> **Time**: 03 Hours | **Maximum Marks**: 60  
> **Instructions**: Answer Question No.1 which is compulsory and any five from the rest. The figures in the right-hand margin indicate marks.

---

## [[MPMC Theory Syllabus]]

- **Module 1**: 8085 Architecture, Machine Cycles, Opcode Fetch Timing Diagram, Interrupts, Addressing Modes.
- **Module 2**: 8086 Architecture, Bus Interface Unit, Execution Unit, Addressing Modes.
- **Module 3**: Peripheral Interfacing (8255 PPI, 8251 USART, ADC 0808/0809 Interfacing).
- **Module 4**: 8051 Microcontroller Pin Configuration, Port Circuits, Timers.
